# Provenance of the Web of Science and Scopus search string

Two different strings for these searches exist in the project archive. The long string, printed in Appendix A of the
manuscript, is the one used; the short string is discarded.

**Used (long string).** Recorded in `search_log.md` on 17 September 2026, together with the result-page URLs, search IDs
and retrieval counts (Scopus 382, Web of Science 352). It was written at the time of the search.

**Discarded (short string).** Found in `S1_scopus_search_strategy.txt`, `S2_wos_search_strategy.txt`,
`exact_search_strategies.txt` and `S1_search_strategies.txt`, which were created on 18-19 September, after the searches.
They carry no counts or URLs and appear to be a later reconstruction.

**Empirical check.** 19 of the 349 Web of Science records with abstracts (5%) match the psychological block only through
terms present in the long string (psychotherapy, coping, relaxation, emotion regulation, acceptance and commitment) and
could not have been returned by the short string.
