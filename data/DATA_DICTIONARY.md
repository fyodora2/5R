# Data dictionary

Dataset for "Randomized Trials of Digital Interventions Reporting Occupational Burnout Outcomes: A Scoping Review and Evidence Map". Repository: https://github.com/fyodora2/5R. All files are UTF-8, comma-separated, one header row. Search dates: 17 to 29 September 2026 (last search run 29 September 2026). Abstracts retrieved from Web of Science and Scopus are not redistributed (database terms of use); full-text articles are not redistributed.

## Identifiers
- `record_id` (R001-R288): one assessed report (article or registry-linked publication). Frozen; never renumbered.
- `study_id` (S001-S119, with gaps): one randomized trial; reports of the same trial share one study_id. Frozen through `study_id_map.csv`; the gaps belong to trials that were excluded after full-text reading.
- `sid` (S/W/T-prefixed screening identifiers): a record in a supplementary-search screening file.

## Category vocabularies
- Occupation (8): Other or mixed healthcare workers; Nurses; Physicians and physician trainees; Teachers and education staff; Mental-health and social-care professionals; Workers selected for a health condition or risk; Employees of a named occupation, industry or enterprise; Mixed-occupation workers recruited across employers.
- Delivery (8): Web-based program; Live online sessions; Smartphone app; Blended (digital and in-person); Text or instant messaging; Wearable or motion-sensing platform; Ambient AI scribe; Chatbot.
- Comparator (5): Waitlist or delayed access; Active or attention control; Usual practice or no intervention; Head-to-head digital variants; Not reported.
- Human support (3): Human-supported or facilitated; Self-guided or automated; Not reported.

## Reproduction
`scripts/final/` rebuilds the ledger, charting, figures and dashboard from the raw files; `paper/build_paper.js` and `paper/build_protocol.js` rebuild the Word files; `paper/make_figures.py` rebuilds the three figures.
