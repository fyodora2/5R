# Randomized Trials of Digital Interventions Reporting Occupational Burnout Outcomes: A Scoping Review and Evidence Map

Short summary of the final results. Full text: [`paper/burnout_scoping_review_paper.docx`](./paper/burnout_scoping_review_paper.docx).

## Method in brief

- **Reporting:** PRISMA-ScR with a PRISMA 2020 flow diagram (databases plus registry-linked "other methods").
- **Sources:** Europe PMC, OpenAlex, ERIC (26 Sep 2026); Web of Science, Scopus (17 Sep 2026); a supplementary search without the intervention-type block in Europe PMC and OpenAlex (28 Sep 2026) and in Web of Science and Scopus (29 Sep 2026); a third supplementary search with a PubMed MeSH check and additional delivery terms in Europe PMC (29 Sep 2026); ClinicalTrials.gov linkage (28 Sep 2026).
- **Eligibility (applied in order):** primary results report, randomized allocation, working population, digital delivery, burnout outcome. The first failed criterion is the recorded reason.
- **Unit of analysis:** the trial. Reports sharing a registration or sample are one study.
- **Quality control:** every report passing screening was assessed against the criteria. A random sample of 45 records excluded in the main search contained no eligible report (exact 95% CI 0 to 7.9%). The three supplementary searches and the registry linkage found further eligible trials the main search had missed (29 trials from the supplementary searches).

## Main findings (n = 116 trials)

- **Time:** 76 trials (65.5%) were first reported in 2023–2026; 16 before 2020.
- **Occupation:** healthcare workers 75; mental-health and social care 7; teachers 14; other sectors 20.
- **Mechanism:** psychological 91; non-psychological 25 (professional training 10, physical activity or health behaviour 6, feedback or navigation 5, workflow automation 3, workplace or practical support 1). Fifteen of the 25 were found only by the supplementary searches.
- **Design:** waitlist 36 (38.2% of trials from 2023), active control 30, usual practice 31, head-to-head 8, not reported 11. Median 119 participants (IQR 69–238).
- **Outcome reporting:** the burnout instrument is named in the abstract in 56 trials (48.3%). Full texts identified it for 56 of the other 60 (four not determined); the Maslach Burnout Inventory or an adaptation was used in 63 trials (54.3%).
- **Registry:** of 15 randomized registrations completed by 2023, 7 have a results report in this review, 2 published outside its scope and 6 (40%) have no located results report.

## Limitations

Selection by one reviewer with an AI agent; a blind second reviewer assessed a sample of 30 reports (agreement 80%, kappa 0.60, 95% CI 0.31–0.89); assessment mostly on abstracts; PsycINFO, Embase and CENTRAL not searched, no citation chasing, and the third search limited to PubMed and Europe PMC (each search added trials the previous one missed, so more probably remain); two reports not retrievable; registry check limited to ClinicalTrials.gov; protocol not pre-registered; 2026 data incomplete.
